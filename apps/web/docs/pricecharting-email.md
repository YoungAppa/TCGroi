Send from your own address to **support@pricecharting.com** (their API contact).

send it yourself.

**Subject:** API data use on whatsthatroi.com — checking redistribution terms

---

Hi,

I run whatsthatroi.com, a free site that estimates the expected value of sealed
trading-card products (Pokémon, One Piece, Magic) by combining published
pull-rate studies with live card and sealed prices. I'm a paying API subscriber
and I'd like to confirm my usage is within your terms before I promote the site.

How I use your data:

- I query the API on a schedule (roughly daily), store the returned prices in my
  own database, and serve pages from that database. No user page-load ever hits
  your API.
- Prices are displayed as one of two selectable sources — yours is labelled
  "eBay (sold)" and PriceCharting is credited by name on every page footer and
  on my methodology page.
- I display individual card and sealed-product prices, and derived aggregates
  (an average card value per rarity tier, and an expected value per pack/box).
- I do not offer bulk export, a price API of my own, or downloadable datasets.

Two questions:

1. Is displaying your prices this way — attributed, cached, alongside a second
   source — within the terms of my subscription?
2. Is there specific attribution wording or a link format you'd prefer? I'm
   happy to change what I currently use.

If any of the above isn't permitted, tell me which part and I'll remove it
rather than argue the point.

## Why this is worth sending

The site's whole pitch is that its numbers are honest and its sources are named.
Redistributing a paid provider's prices without checking the terms is the one
thing that could take the site down after it starts ranking — and asking costs
nothing. If they say no, the site still works: pokemontcg.io (TCGplayer Market)
and Scrydex cover cards, and only sealed prices would need a replacement.
